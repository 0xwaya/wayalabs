# Ideal customer profile

Internal. Phase 4 of the scale-up plan. Win more of the same buyer.

## Primary — local service / trade

- Quotes, permits, sales inbox, contractor or homeowner leads
- Needs intake or chat → email → CRM → one approved job
- Proof: Urban Stone Collective
- Sell: Discovery → Lead system (flagship)

## Secondary — local media / listings

- Calendars, submissions, operator-reviewed content
- Needs site + forms + data store + handoff
- Proof: Queen City Soundboard
- Sell: Discovery → Platform build

## Do not prioritize

- General “AI consulting” buyers with no operator system
- Buyers who only want an unscoped “AI agent” with no operator system — route them through discovery or no-go
- Categories outside these ICPs until there are 4–5 client case studies in-ICP

## Site and outbound rule

Homepage, offers, and outreach lead with these two patterns. Research (Echo, Grok operators, CEO orchestrator) stays labeled research and is not the primary CTA.
