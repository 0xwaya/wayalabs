# Lead system kit

Internal. Productized shape for the Lead system offer ($7,000–$14,000). Customize knowledge, CRM fields, and the one approved job — not the whole architecture.

Proof reference: Urban Stone Collective (`urbanstone.co`).
Studio self-build: `wayalabs-lead-engine.md` (Wayalabs.com intake).

## Fixed shape

1. **Intake** — quote form and/or on-site chat
2. **Email** — lead lands in the operator inbox (Resend or equivalent)
3. **CRM** — private record after email succeeds; match by email, phone, or company when possible
4. **One scheduled job** — operator-approved; does not contact people who did not opt in unless the brief says otherwise

## Discovery inputs required

- Outcome and who it is for
- Systems in scope / out of scope
- Data boundary: what is stored, who can read it, retention
- Knowledge sources the chat may use (approved only)
- Human escalation path
- The single scheduled job, if any

## Delivery checklist

- [ ] Form or chat posts to email, then CRM
- [ ] Chat grounded in approved material only; handoff works
- [ ] CRM match rules documented for the operator
- [ ] Scheduled job documented with owner and schedule
- [ ] Operator can run the system without Wayalabs in the loop
- [ ] Close with Watch invitation (see `close-checklist.md`)

## Pricing band

Price inside $7,000–$14,000 by complexity of knowledge, CRM fields, and the job — not by inventing new product lines mid-build.
