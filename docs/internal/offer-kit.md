# WayaLabs offer kit

Internal. Do not publish this ladder. The public page is [/offers](https://wayalabs.com/offers). It may show the discovery list price and the new-customer price only.

Updated 3 November 2026. These are operator list prices, not a record of booked revenue.

## New-customer rule

40% off the first paid invoice for an organization that has not paid WayaLabs before.

- One discount. One invoice. Then the standard rate.
- If discovery is the first invoice, discovery is discounted and the later build is not.
- If the first invoice is a platform or a lead system, that invoice is discounted and discovery is not also discounted.
- Care retainers are never discounted.
- Agent pilots are not for sale.
- The invoice names the list amount, the 40% line, and the amount due.
- A predecessor organization that already paid does not qualify.

## Ladder

| Offer | Buyer gets | List | First invoice at 40% off | After that |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Discovery sprint | 1 week. Written brief, systems in scope, data boundary, go or no-go | $3,500 | $2,100 | Standard rate if this was not the intro invoice |
| Platform build | Site, forms, email, data store, handoff | $15,000–$28,000 | $9,000–$16,800, only when this is the first invoice | List |
| Lead system | Intake or chat, email, CRM, one scheduled job | $8,000–$16,000 | $4,800–$9,600, only when this is the first invoice | List |
| Care retainer | Fixes, monitoring, small monthly changes | $2,000 / month | Not eligible | List |
| Agent pilot | Scoped prototype with human approval | Unlisted | Do not sell | Do not sell |

A lead system can be sold alone or added to a platform build. Quote the add-on inside the same first invoice only when that combined invoice is the organization's first payment. Do not take 40% off discovery and then take 40% off the build.

## Discovery deposit

Half of the amount due, before the working session.

- New customer: $1,050 of the $2,100 sprint.
- Returning customer: $1,750 of the $3,500 sprint.

## What each offer is allowed to claim

Discovery produces a written brief. It does not promise a metric.

Platform and lead-system quotes have to match a shipped pattern: a site, forms, email, a data store, and, when sold, one chat or one scheduled job with a human handoff. Urban Stone Collective and Queen City Soundboard are the references. Do not attach support-deflection rates, conversion rates, or response-time claims.

Care starts the month after a launch the studio shipped. It is not a discount plan.

The CEO orchestrator and the private Echo runtime stay off the invoice.

## Invoice line

`New customer discount, 40% off the first engagement`

Show the list amount above that line. If the buyer is not new, omit the line.
