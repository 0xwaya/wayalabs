# WayaLabs offer kit

Internal. Do not publish this ladder. The public page is [/offers](https://wayalabs.com/offers). It may show the discovery prices and the 60-day credit only.

Updated 3 November 2026. Operator list prices, set under published one-week and custom-site bands. Not booked revenue.

## Rules

40% off the first paid invoice for an organization that has not paid WayaLabs before.

- One discount. One invoice.
- If discovery is that invoice, a platform or lead-system build started within 60 days gets the discovery amount paid as a credit. The build is not also reduced by 40%.
- If the first invoice is the build, that invoice is discounted and there is no discovery credit.
- Watch and build-block plans are never discounted.
- Agent pilots are not for sale.
- A predecessor organization that already paid does not qualify.

## Ladder

|---|---|---:|---:|---|

| Watch | Monitoring and small fixes | $400 / month | Not eligible | List |
| Build block | A scheduled block of build changes | $1,200 / month | Not eligible | List |
| Agent pilot | Scoped prototype | Unlisted | Do not sell | Do not sell |

Do not discount discovery and the build. Credit replaces the second discount.

## Deposit

Half of the discovery amount due, before the working session.

- New customer: $600 of $1,200.
- Returning customer: $1,000 of $2,000.

## Invoice lines

`New customer discount, 40% off the first engagement`

`Discovery credit, fee paid on [date]`

Show the list amount, then only the line that applies.
